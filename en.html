WHO WE AREWho We Are

ARIA is a Tokyo-based corporate group that conceives, launches, and operates its own businesses across three core domains.

We identify untapped potential and nurture it into the essential businesses of the future.

WHAT WE DOWhat We Do

ARIA currently operates across three core domains: Commerce & FinTech, Education & HR, and Legal Services.
While these fields differ, they share a single underlying philosophy: we never accept the status quo. Instead, we rebuild systems for the better.

WHAT WE DO

COMMERCE & FINTECH

Alongside e-commerce, resale, and the trading of both digital and physical goods, we are developing next-generation payment and financial technology services.

EDUCATION & TALENT

We connect ambitious individuals with new opportunities, providing platforms and programs that fuel their growth.
By challenging conventional thinking around education and careers, we create an environment where people can pursue new paths based purely on their true abilities and potential.

LEGAL SERVICES

We provide specialized services and support in the legal sector.
Our goal is to cut through the complexity of laws and regulations, creating a seamless system where individuals and businesses can easily access the vital information and support they need.

For services requiring specific professional qualifications, we strictly comply with all relevant laws and regulations, partnering with specialized experts as necessary.

HOW WE WORKHow We Work

ARIA’s ability to conceive, launch, and bring new businesses to the world is driven by three core working principles.

01

EYE FOR POTENTIALAn Eye For Potential

We discover hidden opportunities and untapped potential before anyone else.

02

EMBRACE TECHNOLOGYTechnological Agility

We believe technology expands human capability. That is why we proactively embrace new technologies as soon as they emerge.

03

READ THE SIGNSAnticipating Trends

By the time a shift becomes obvious to everyone, the opportunity is already lost. We read the early signals while markets are still in their infancy, designing the businesses that will be needed next.

Find potential, scale with technology, and shape the future.
The intersection of these three forces is exactly what defines ARIA.
